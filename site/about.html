About JohorStudio.com

A professional digital media provider since 2014, based in Bandar Putra, Kulai.

Technical by nature

We started with a passion for cameras and grew into live production. Today we run multicamera shows, livestreams and hybrid events for government agencies, hospitals, universities and companies.

Our core team is small and hands-on, and we bring in trusted crew for larger events. The person who plans your event is the person running the switcher on the day.

We work closely with your organiser, from the site visit to the hand-over, and we deliver edited photos in one to three days and full recordings of every stream.

How a job runs

  1. 1. WhatsApp us your date and venue

    We reply with questions about your programme and screens.

  2. 2. Quotation and deposit

    A written quotation, then a 30 to 50 percent deposit to lock the date.

  3. 3. Site visit for large events

    We check power, internet, camera positions and the LED feed before the day.

  4. 4. Event day and hand-over

    Same-day edit if booked, photos in one to three days, recordings delivered through our private cloud.
