An afternoon-to-night Maulidur Rasul programme at Masjid Saleng. The mosque hall was dressed with a stage backdrop and floral arrangements for the ceramah, while volunteers prepared food for the congregation outside.

Our coverage followed the guest speakers, the audience, the kitchen and serving teams, and the group photos of the committee and volunteers at the end of the night.
