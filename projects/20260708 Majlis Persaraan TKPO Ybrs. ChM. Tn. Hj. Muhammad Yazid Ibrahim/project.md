A retirement dinner, "Noktah Persaraan, Mengukir Kenangan", to honour the retiring Deputy Director General (Operations). Colleagues filled the hall in black and gold, with speeches, a live band, songs from the guest of honour and plenty of laughter.

We covered the arrivals, the speeches and performances, the dinner tables and the many group photos at the floral photo corner.
