The 12th convocation of SMPK Vokasional Indahpura. Our team covered the stage presentations, the academic procession, the gamelan ensemble and the guests.

Graduates and families queued at our book-wall photo booth for portraits, and we finished with group photos of each course and of the teaching staff.
