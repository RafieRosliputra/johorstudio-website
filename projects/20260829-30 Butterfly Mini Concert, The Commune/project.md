A two-day mini concert and carnival, "A Stage to Shine, a Day to Celebrate", in a busy mall atrium. Children performed on a themed stage, then received certificates with their teachers, while families enjoyed the photo booth and activities around the hall.

We covered every performance, the award presentations and the families and teachers behind the show.
