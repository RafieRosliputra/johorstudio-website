A full day of Maulidur Rasul celebrations for the Taman Skudai Kanan community. The programme opened with a tazkirah and doa inside the surau, followed by a colourful street procession with banners, flags and bunga manggar through the neighbourhood.

In the evening the stage came alive with selawat, qasidah and a guest speaker in front of a packed crowd. We covered the talks, the procession, the guests of honour and the warm moments between the congregation.
